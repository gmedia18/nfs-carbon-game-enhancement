## Configuration

Tuning is where the real racers separate themselves. Need for Speed Carbon gives you deep control over how your machine feels and performs — here's how to dial it in without wasting a lap.

**Graphics & Performance**

- Match your visual settings to your hardware first. On older rigs, drop shadows and reflections before touching texture quality — you'll keep the frame rate smooth and the cars still look sharp.
- Lock your resolution to your monitor's native value. Stretching the image costs frames and gains you nothing.
- If you hit stutter in busy city sections, cap the frame rate. A steady 60 beats a jittery 90 every time.

**Control Setup**

- Bind drift and handbrake where your fingers already live. Default layouts work, but muscle memory from your favorite racer is faster.
- Keyboard players: remap steering to your dominant hand and keep both hands on acceleration and braking. Controller players: dial in dead zones before you touch sensitivity.
- Test every change on a short circuit, not mid-career. Two minutes of practice saves a restart.

**Car Tuning**

- Tune per event, not per car. Grip builds dominate tight street circuits; drift builds eat up canyon runs for breakfast.
- Adjust gearing to favor acceleration on twisty tracks and top speed on long straights. One setup will not win both.
- Weight reduction and tire compounds return more lap time than raw horsepower in the early game. Spend smart.

**Crew & Career Settings**

- Assign crew roles to match your driving style — a drafter for aggressive point-to-point races, a blocker when you need breathing room.
- Review your garage loadout before every territory takeover. Wrong car, wrong night, guaranteed loss.
- Keep a dedicated drift car in the garage. Switching builds mid-career costs money and momentum.

**Save & Profile**

- Back up your save data before big tuning sessions. One corrupted profile is a whole career gone.
- Keep separate profiles for casual runs and serious campaigns so your progression stays clean.
- Verify your game files after any major update — a corrupted config tanking your frame rate is a rookie mistake you won't make twice.

Configure once, race forever. Get the setup right and the streets of Palmont are yours.

## FAQ

**Does Need for Speed Carbon run on modern Windows?**

Yes. It's built for Windows 7 and up on x64 or x86, and the current release ships with the optimizations needed to keep races smooth on today's hardware. If you're on an older rig, you're still covered — 512 MB of RAM and a 1.5 GHz processor is all it takes to get on the road.

**Do I need a high-end PC to play?**

No. This is one of the most accessible racers in the series. With just 4 GB of free storage and modest CPU and RAM requirements, it runs on everyday machines without breaking a sweat. Performance stays clean, and the drift mechanics feel tight even on low-spec setups.

**What makes the crew system worth using?**

Your crew isn't cosmetic — it's strategy. Drafters pull you through the pack, scouts find shortcuts, and blockers shut down rivals. Pick the right mix for each race type and you gain a real tactical edge. Ignore it, and you'll feel the difference on the leaderboard.

**Can I customize my cars?**

Absolutely. Win races, bank your earnings, and pour them into unlocks and upgrades. Every part you swap changes how the car performs on the road, so tuning isn't just for looks — it's how you stay competitive as the story arc escalates.

**Is drifting hard to learn?**

It's the core skill, and it rewards practice. The handling and drift mechanics are tuned for responsive control, so once you get the timing down, you'll be chaining drifts through corners and shaving seconds off your runs. Put in the laps — it pays off.

**How does the story arc fit into the racing?**

Every race pushes the narrative forward. Cinematic cutscenes keep you invested between events, and the campaign gives each win real weight. You're not just racing for times — you're racing through a story, and the stakes climb as you go.

**What's included in the current version?**

The full game: complete campaign, crew system, car customization, drift mechanics, and mini-challenges. Recent updates refined vehicle handling and drift dynamics, balanced the crew system, and stabilized online matches. Always run the latest version — older builds miss these fixes.

**Is there multiplayer?**

Yes. Online matches are part of the package, with improved connectivity and match stability in the current release. Test your builds and drift lines against other racers, or stay offline and grind the campaign and challenges at your own pace.

**Do I need an internet connection?**

Only for online play and updates. The campaign, customization, and mini-challenges are all playable offline. Connect when you want to race others or pull down the newest patch.

**How do I unlock new cars?**

Race, win, and spend. Your winnings fuel unlocks across the roster, and each new ride opens fresh customization paths. The faster you climb the story arc, the deeper the garage gets.
